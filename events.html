About the Conference

Innovation, Sustainability
& Waste Repurposing

The primary focus of the annual conference is Innovation, Sustainability, and Waste Repurposing. These interconnected areas have received increasing attention globally across academia, government, and the private sector, reflecting growing recognition of the need for approaches that support environmental sustainability and contribute to the achievement of the Sustainable Development Goals.

Abstract Process, Publication & Participation

Info on Abstract Submission

How abstracts
are reviewed

All abstracts undergo an initial screening by the internal committee to assess their alignment with the conference scope and requirements.

1

Submission

Abstracts are submitted once registration opens.

2

Initial Screening

The internal committee checks alignment with the conference scope and requirements.

3

Evaluation

Overall evaluation and, where applicable, reviewer feedback.

4

Decision

The committee recommends acceptance, rejection, or revision.

To be Published in the Journal of Resource Valorization and Circular Systems (JRVCS)

Accepted contributions are expected to receive DOI assignment, providing a formal record of accepted conference papers and enhancing their accessibility and visibility to the academic and research community. Subject to the journal's publication and indexing processes.
